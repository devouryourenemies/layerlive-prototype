Competitor map

The market is crowded, but the exact lane is still open.

Competitor What they own Weakness we attack Our better angle
Figma / FigJam Realtime UI, product design, whiteboarding Vector/UI-first, not Photoshop-style raster painting Raster-first creative rooms with layers, brushes, image edits, PSD-style workflows
Canva Template design, simple team collaboration Great for non-designers, weaker for pro editing control Creator/pro mode, custom brushes, asset stacks, speed-focused art direction
Miro Whiteboards, workshops, planning boards Not built for finished visual production Canvas collaboration that ends in exportable production assets
Adobe Creative Cloud Industry-standard creative tools Collaboration is file/comment/review-heavy, less room-native Lightweight browser rooms that can later plug into Adobe workflows
Photopea Browser Photoshop clone Powerful editor, not known as multiplayer-first Same browser accessibility, but collaboration is the product core
Aggie.io / Magma Collaborative drawing and sketch sessions Often art/sketch-focused, not agency/team workflow-focused Design ops: versions, approvals, client rooms, project handoff, team permissions
